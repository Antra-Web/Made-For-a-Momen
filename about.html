We believe a gift should feel like a memory.

our story

One table. One idea.

It started at a kitchen table with leftover ribbon and a candle nobody had asked for. A birthday gift, made because a store-bought one didn't feel like enough. The reaction it got — that was the whole business plan.

A few handmade gifts became a few dozen. A spare room became a studio. And a small community of people who'd rather give something made slowly than something picked quickly grew up around it.

Today, every piece that leaves our studio is still touched by hand — measured, wrapped, and checked by someone who cares whether it lands right.

How we got here.

2021

A kitchen table, leftover ribbon, and one gift made for a friend's birthday.

2022

Word spreads. Orders outgrow the kitchen table — the first real studio space.

2024

A small team joins — each one trained by hand, the way the founder learned.

2026

Thousands of gifts later, still wrapped one at a time, by hand.
